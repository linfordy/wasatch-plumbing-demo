## Plumbing Emergencies That Require a 24-Hour Plumber in Garland

Not every plumbing problem is an emergency. But these situations always are — and waiting until morning will make them significantly worse and more expensive.

### Burst or Ruptured Pipes

A ruptured supply line can dump 100+ gallons of water into your home per hour. Garland, TX homes — particularly those with older galvanized or copper supply lines — are vulnerable to bursts during temperature swings. Salt Lake Valley doesn't freeze often, but when it does, pipes inside exterior walls and in unconditioned spaces are at serious risk.

If a pipe bursts, locate your main water shutoff valve immediately (typically near the street meter or inside the garage) and shut it off before calling your [emergency plumber](/services/emergency-plumbing).

### Sewer Line Backup

If multiple drains in your Garland home back up at the same time — toilets, tubs, and sinks all draining slowly or not at all — you likely have a main sewer line blockage or failure. This is a health emergency. Raw sewage backing up into a home carries pathogens and causes contamination that requires professional remediation.

Older Garland neighborhoods have clay or cast iron sewer lines that can collapse, crack, or become heavily invaded by tree roots. This is a situation that requires a 24-hour plumber, not a wait-until-morning call.

### Water Heater Failure with Active Flooding

Water heater tanks fail by leaking slowly — or catastrophically. A bottom-seam tank failure can release 40–80 gallons of water into your utility space rapidly. If your [water heater](/services/water-heaters) is leaking actively, turn off the cold water supply valve at the top of the tank and call immediately. Don't wait.

### Gas Line Concerns Near Plumbing

If you smell gas anywhere near your water heater, furnace, or gas supply lines — leave the house, don't touch any switches, and call your gas utility and a licensed plumber. Natural gas work in Texas must be performed by a licensed plumber (not just an HVAC tech or handyman).

### Overflowing Toilets with No Shutoff

If the toilet shutoff valve behind the base of the toilet is corroded and won't turn — and the toilet is overflowing — go straight to the main house shutoff. This is more common in older Garland, TX homes with valves that haven't been touched in 20+ years.

## What to Do Before the 24-Hour Plumber Arrives in Garland

The 15–30 minutes between your call and the technician's arrival matter. These steps reduce damage:

**1. Shut off the water supply.**
For isolated problems (toilet, sink, washing machine), use the local shutoff valve. For burst pipes or major flooding, go to the main shutoff — typically at the meter near the street.

**2. Turn off the water heater.**
If you've shut off the main supply, turn off your water heater (gas: turn the dial to "Pilot"; electric: shut the breaker). Running a water heater dry damages the element and tank.

**3. Document the damage.**
Take photos and video of the damage before cleanup begins. Your homeowner's insurance in Garland, TX may cover sudden plumbing failures — and documentation is essential for any claim.

**4. Move valuables and electronics.**
If water is actively spreading, move items off the floor in adjacent rooms. Water travels fast under doors and through flooring.

**5. Don't use any drains.**
If you suspect a sewer line backup, do not flush toilets or run any water. Every gallon you add makes the backup worse.
