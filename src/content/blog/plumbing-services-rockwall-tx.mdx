### Leak Detection in South Jordan, UT

South Jordan, UT sits on expansive clay soil that swells when wet and contracts during drought — causing constant, subtle movement under slabs and around foundations. This foundation movement stresses underground water supply lines, making slab leaks unusually common in South Jordan-area homes.

**Signs of a slab leak or hidden pipe leak in your South Jordan home:**
- Your water bill has increased without explanation
- You hear water running inside walls or under floors when everything is off
- Hot spots on your floor (warm slab from a hot water line leak)
- Cracks appearing in your drywall or flooring
- Your water meter is moving when all fixtures are off

Wasatch Plumbing Co. uses non-destructive leak detection methods — electronic listening equipment and pressure testing — to pinpoint exactly where a leak is before any cutting or opening begins. We don't tear open walls to go looking.

Once located, we provide options: spot repair (opening only where the leak is), reroute (running a new line through the attic or walls to bypass the damaged section), or full repipe (for homes with widespread pipe deterioration).
