Slow drains are the most common plumbing call we receive in South Jordan, UT. Hair, grease, soap scum, mineral scale from hard water, and tree root intrusion all cause blockages that household chemicals can't touch. We use three professional-grade methods:

- **Hydro-jetting:** High-pressure water (up to 4,000 PSI) scours the pipe interior clean — roots, grease, and scale removed completely. The gold standard for recurring drain problems.
