### Water Heater Problems

South Jordan, UT has hard water — high mineral content that accelerates sediment buildup inside traditional tank-style water heaters. This shortens heater lifespan, reduces efficiency, and eventually causes the rumbling sounds and cold-water surprises that send homeowners searching for a plumber in South Jordan, UT at the worst times.

We handle [water heater repair in South Jordan, UT](/blog/water-heater-repair-rockwall-tx) for both tank and tankless units. If your unit is under 8 years old and the problem is an isolated component — thermocouple, heating element, anode rod — repair is almost always the right call. If it's 10+ years old and leaking from the tank body, replacement makes more financial sense.

### Leak Detection and Slab Leaks

Slab leaks are a significant concern in South Jordan, UT. The region's expansive clay soils shift seasonally, placing stress on supply lines running under concrete foundations. A slab leak that goes undetected for weeks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and water bills that spike by hundreds of dollars a month.

Signs you may have a slab leak in your South Jordan, UT home:
- Water bill increases with no change in usage
- Warm or hot spots on tile or wood floors
- Sound of running water when everything is shut off
- Cracks appearing in baseboards or drywall
