### Drain Problems

The single most common reason South Jordan, UT residents call a plumber. South Jordan sits in one of the hardest water markets in Salt Lake Valley — calcium and mineral deposits build up inside drain walls over time, narrowing flow until you get that familiar slow-drain or standing-water problem. Hair, grease, soap scum, and tree roots compound the issue.

What a plumber in South Jordan, UT will do:
- **Camera inspection first** — a waterproof camera goes into the drain before any work begins, so you're paying to fix a confirmed problem, not a guess
- **Hydro-jetting** for stubborn blockages — high-pressure water (up to 4,000 PSI) scours the pipe interior completely clean

### Leak Detection & Slab Leaks

Hidden leaks are the most expensive plumbing problem a South Jordan, UT homeowner can face — not because repairs are complicated, but because they go undetected for months while doing structural damage and driving water bills up 20–40%.

Signs you may have a hidden leak:
- Water bill suddenly 20%+ higher with no change in usage
- Warm or damp spots on the floor (possible slab leak)
- Sound of running water when all fixtures are off
- Mold smell coming from walls or under cabinets
- Visible foundation cracks appearing faster than normal

Slab leaks are particularly common in South Jordan, UT because Salt Lake Valley clay soils expand and contract aggressively with seasonal moisture changes, putting constant stress on pipes embedded in the foundation.
