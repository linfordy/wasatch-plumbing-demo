## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if a plumber is licensed in Utah?

Utah requires all plumbers to hold a license issued by the Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing (DOPL). You can verify a contractor's license number on the DOPL website; an active license confirms the plumber has passed a trade exam and maintains insurance. Unlicensed plumbers may offer lower prices but create liability if work fails or causes water damage.

### What should I do if I have a plumbing emergency at night or on a weekend?

Call your local plumber's emergency line; most South Jordan contractors offer 24/7 dispatch for burst pipes, gas leaks, or sewage backups. Emergency calls typically include a service fee ($50–$100) in addition to labor and parts. Temporary measures—shutting off the main water valve, using a plunger, or applying heat tape to frozen pipes—can prevent damage until the technician arrives.

### How often should I have my water heater serviced?

Water heaters benefit from annual flushing to remove sediment buildup, which extends tank life by 2–3 years. Most tanks last 8–12 years; if yours is older than 10 years and leaking, replacement is more cost-effective than repair. A technician can inspect the anode rod and thermostat during a service call to catch failures early.

### What's the difference between a plumber and a handyman for minor repairs?

Licensed plumbers can legally perform any plumbing work, including gas line installation and code-compliant repairs. Handymen may handle simple tasks like replacing a faucet aerator but cannot legally install new fixtures or modify water lines in most jurisdictions. For any work affecting water supply or drainage, hire a licensed plumber to ensure code compliance and warranty coverage.

### Can frozen pipes be prevented in South Jordan winters?

Yes. Insulate exposed pipes in unheated spaces (crawl spaces, attics, garages) with foam sleeves or heat tape. Allow faucets to drip slowly during freezing nights to maintain water flow. Seal air leaks around pipes and keep cabinet doors open under sinks to allow warm air circulation. These steps prevent 90% of freeze-ups in South Jordan homes.

### How much does a slab leak repair cost?

Slab leak repair ranges from $1,500 to $4,000+ depending on location and whether the concrete must be cut. Camera inspection ($300–$600) identifies the leak location before repair begins. Some leaks can be rerouted through walls above the slab, reducing cost; others require concrete cutting and pipe replacement. Insurance may cover water damage but typically not the repair itself.
