## Common Plumbing Problems in South Jordan Homes

South Jordan's climate and older housing stock create predictable plumbing issues. Hard water buildup affects pipes and fixtures; mineral deposits reduce water pressure and shorten water heater lifespan. Frozen pipes occur during winter months, particularly in uninsulated crawl spaces. Sewer line damage from tree roots is common in established neighborhoods. Leaking faucets, running toilets, and water heater failures account for the majority of service calls. Emergency services—burst pipes, backed-up drains, no-hot-water situations—typically cost $150–$300 for a service call plus parts and labor.
