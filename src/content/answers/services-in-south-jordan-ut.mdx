We work with property managers, restaurants, medical offices, and light industrial facilities to minimize downtime and prevent costly emergency repairs.

## Water Heater Services

Water heater repair and replacement represent a significant portion of South Jordan service calls. Tank-style water heaters typically last 8–12 years before requiring replacement; tankless systems offer longer lifespans but higher upfront costs. Wasatch Plumbing diagnoses issues like insufficient hot water, sediment buildup, and corrosion, then recommends repair or replacement based on the unit's age and condition. Installation includes proper venting, pressure relief valve setup, and code compliance.

## Drain Cleaning and Sewer Line Services

Clogged drains and sewer backups are common in South Jordan's mix of older and newer homes. Wasatch Plumbing uses video inspection to identify blockages, tree root intrusion, and structural damage in sewer lines. Drain cleaning methods range from mechanical snaking to hydro-jetting, depending on the severity and location of the clog. Sewer line replacement may be necessary if roots have damaged the line or if the line has collapsed.
