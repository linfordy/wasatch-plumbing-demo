## Common Plumbing Problems in South Jordan Homes

**Water heater failure** is the most frequent service call in the region. Tank water heaters typically last 8–12 years; South Jordan's mineral content accelerates sediment buildup, reducing efficiency. Tankless and hybrid models are increasingly common replacements.

**Frozen pipes** occur November through March when temperatures drop below 20°F. Uninsulated lines in crawl spaces, exterior walls, or attics freeze first. Prevention costs $200–$600 for insulation; emergency thaw-out and repair runs $400–$1,500 if the pipe bursts.

**Slab leaks** develop in homes built on concrete foundations when supply lines corrode or shift. Detection requires electronic leak detection equipment; repair involves either rerouting lines above-slab or spot excavation. Costs range from $1,500 to $4,000 depending on location and extent.

**Drain clogs** from hair, grease, and mineral buildup are routine. Snaking costs $150–$300; hydro-jetting (high-pressure water cleaning) runs $300–$600 and is more effective for stubborn buildup.
