### Water Heaters (Tank)
Same-day water heater repair, installation, and replacement. Gas and electric. All installations include proper venting, code-compliant connections, new drain pan, and safety check. Standard tank replacement takes 2–3 hours.

**Sizing guide:**
- 40-gallon → 1–2 people
- 50-gallon → 3–4 people
- 75+ gallon or tankless → larger families

**Signs you need replacement:** no hot water, rust-colored water, popping/banging noises, leaking around the base, age over 10–12 years.

## Emergency Protocol (Important)

If a caller describes any of the following, treat as URGENT and route them to call (214) 729-3586 immediately:

- Burst pipe / flooding
- No water in the home
- Sewage backing up
- Gas leak (also advise them to leave the house first)
- Water heater leaking or making loud noises
- Slab leak (warm spots on floor, sound of running water with everything off)

**Pre-arrival guidance to share with emergency callers:**
- **Water leak:** Locate the main water shutoff valve and turn it off.
- **Gas leak:** Leave the house immediately. Call from outside.
- **Sewer backup:** Avoid using any drains, toilets, or running water until we arrive.

- The customer is describing an emergency
- The job sounds complex or unusual (large commercial, repiping, major remodel)
