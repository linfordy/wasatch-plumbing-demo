## STEP 1 — Open the Tech App (20 seconds)

Open Safari on your phone → type: **proofpop.ai/tech/wasatch-plumbing**

> "This is the technician's interface. No app to download, no login — just a URL. We send it to every tech on the team. Works on any phone."

Show the screen: name of the company, record button.

---

## STEP 2 — Record a Capture (60 seconds)

Hit the record button and say something like:

> *"Just finished installing a new 50-gallon water heater for a homeowner in South Jordan. The old unit was about 12 years old, starting to leak from the bottom — classic end-of-life. Took about two hours including haul-away. Upgraded the supply lines to braided stainless while we were in there and brought the gas line up to code. Customer was super happy, said they'd been dealing with lukewarm water for months."*

Stop recording. Attach 1-2 photos (pre-saved job photos on your phone work great).

Hit **Publish**.

---

## STEP 3 — Wait Beat (15 seconds)

While it processes:

> "ProofPop is now doing three things in parallel."

> "First: it's running the voice transcript through AI — stripping any PII like the customer's last name or exact address, so we never accidentally publish something we shouldn't."

> "Second: it's generating SEO-optimized content from the transcript. Real sentences, real location signals, the kind of content Google loves."

> "Third: it's pushing that content — with the photos — directly to the website."

---

## STEP 4 — The Reveal (30 seconds)

On your phone or a shared screen, open: **wasatch-plumbing-demo.vercel.app/recent-work**

Refresh the page.

> "That just happened. From my phone. In under a minute. That job is now a published page on this website, indexed by Google, with schema markup, the tech's name, the location, the photos — everything Google needs to rank it."

Tap into the new page. Show the polished content + photos.

> "And it happened automatically. The tech just talked for 30 seconds. ProofPop did the rest."

## Objection Handling

**"Will techs actually use it?"**
> "That's exactly why the reward system exists. When the app is this simple — just talk and take a photo — and they get $10 in their pocket automatically, adoption is high. Larry's Plumbing (our first customer) went from zero to capturing every job within a week."

**"Does it really rank on Google?"**
